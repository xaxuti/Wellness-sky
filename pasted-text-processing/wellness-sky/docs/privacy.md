# Privacy and safety

Wellness Sky stores only the account information needed to provide the app.
Reflections are optional; private notes are encrypted at rest with a key
derived from `WELLNESS_SECRET_KEY`. Set that secret in a deployment environment
and keep backups protected. Do not commit the database or production secret.

The platform is not an emergency service or a clinical tool. If someone may be
in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a crisis line now.
